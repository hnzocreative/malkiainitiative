Investor Inquiries

How does the 1,000-Day Sprint connect to the $2M plan? +

The 1,000-Day Sprint is our initial acceleration push requiring $900,000 to resource 12,000 girls immediately, serving as the catalyst for the wider 5-year $2,000,000 institutional strategy.

What makes the $75 unit cost sustainable? +

By leveraging existing public schools, local polytechnics (MNP), and community volunteer mentors (Mentors on the Road), we eliminate bloated overhead while maintaining direct impact.

How will progress be monitored? +

Progress is evaluated quarterly and overseen by our MERL Manager against the 2025–2029 Logical Framework, with a formal mid-term evaluation scheduled for mid-2027.
