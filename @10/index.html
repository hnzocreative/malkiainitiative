How are donor funds audited and allocated? +

In 2025, total revenue was $310,002.18 against $302,420.37 expenditure. 81.4% ($246,304.12) went directly to grassroots program delivery, with only 18.6% allocated to operations and admin[cite: 8].

What is the unit cost to sponsor one girl? +

Under the 1,000-Day Agency Framework, comprehensive annual support (scholarship, dignity kits, SRHR guidance, and mentorship) costs exactly $75 per girl.

Who leads The Malkia Initiative? +

Founded in 2015 by Jedidah Lemaron, Malkia is an authentic youth-led, women-led community non-profit headquartered at ACK Tenebo House in Kajiado Central, Kenya.

The Big Idea

"If the solution does not reach her, it is not a solution yet."

Conventional development treats adolescent girls as passive recipients of aid. Over the next decade, Malkia positions them as co-designers, researchers, and economic owners. From our Olgulului Resource Center to in-school Crown Clubs, we build community systems where girls do not merely survive harmful cultural traditions—they reshape them.
