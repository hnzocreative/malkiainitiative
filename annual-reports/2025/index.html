Audited Telemetry

Independent Auditor Opinion

The 2025 financial statements were independently audited in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (ISA), issuing an unqualified opinion on compliance and internal control.

IAO Question: How is overhead managed?

Overhead and administrative costs are strictly capped under 20% (18.6% in 2025), ensuring more than four-fifths of all funding is channeled directly to frontline activities.

IAO Question: How are field results verified?

Through student term registries, polytechnic attendance tracking, community chief logs, and bi-annual MERL assessments.
