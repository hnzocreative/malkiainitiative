Pillar 01 • TVET

SheRISE Vocational Track

Partnering with Maasai National Polytechnic to equip adolescent mothers and survivors of child marriage with accredited cosmetology, hairdressing, and micro-enterprise toolkits.

  • 100% of 2025 cohort graduated with toolkits
  • Micro-salon salon collectives launched
  • Childcare crèches integrated during training
Pillar 02 • SRHR

Voices Uncut & Pad Banks

County-wide menstrual equity, school Crown Clubs, and adolescent health mentorship dismantling silence surrounding SRHR and forced cross-border unions.

  • 3,800 dignity packages supplied in 2025
  • 22 active school-based Crown Club hubs
  • 5-Ward traditional elder mediation pacts
Pillar 03 • Literacy

KUZA Literacy (TaRL Pilot)

Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) methodology accelerating phonetic decoding and numeracy for girls at risk of academic dropout due to seasonal migration.

  • 324 learners reached story-level fluency
  • 10-week accelerated phonetics bootcamps
  • 92.4% primary school retention recorded
