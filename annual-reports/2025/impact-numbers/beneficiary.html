MERL Telemetry

Data Collection Methodology

All field metrics are captured directly by community mentors, peer leaders, and headteachers using mobile ODK survey forms and verified against school admission rolls.

Retention Verification Standards

Education retention is tracked term-over-term. A learner is confirmed retained only when registered for end-of-term examinations with at least 85% logged classroom attendance.

Independent Sampling

Our internal MERL department conducts bi-annual unannounced spot-checks across 20% of partner institutions to audit supply ledgers and attendance logs.
