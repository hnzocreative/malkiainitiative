Institutional Registration & Status

The Malkia Initiative is a registered Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) in Kenya under Section 10 of the NGO Coordination Act. 501(c)(3) equivalency determinations are available upon institutional request.

Fiduciary & Anti-Corruption Controls

All institutional grants are received into segregated program bank accounts subject to dual-signatory mandates, automated reconciliation, and zero-tolerance safeguarding standards.

Reporting Timelines

Institutional partners receive quarterly narrative dispatches, bi-annual financial statements, and audited year-end closeouts within 60 days of fiscal year termination.
