Theory of Change Validation

Why Our Work Matters: From Pity to Sovereignty.

Conventional humanitarian models view adolescent pastoralist girls through the lens of victimization. Malkia’s Theory of Change reframes them as co-designers, rights-holders, and economic creators. Here is the empirical proof from 2025.

Lesson 01

Migration Resilience

Drought cycles force pastoral families to migrate across county lines. Fixed-classroom retention collapses unless tied to portable sanitary supplies (Pad Banks) and flexible radio/community tutoring circles.

Lesson 02

Elders as Primary Allies

Rescuing a girl without elder sanction creates community ostracism. When traditional leaders are brought into alternative rites dialogues as co-defenders, cross-border FGM drops by over 50%.

Lesson 03

Economic Finality

Advocacy without livelihood leads teen mothers back into forced arrangements. Vocational toolkits and accredited TVET certification convert rescued minors into financially self-sufficient family breadwinners.

Systems-Level Transformation

Beyond Direct Aid: Reshaping the Policy Canvas

Direct aid saves lives today; systemic policy shift saves generations tomorrow. In 2025, The Malkia Initiative leveraged 10 years of lived-experience research to influence institutional norms at both the county and national levels.
