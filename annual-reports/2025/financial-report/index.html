Fiscal Telemetry

Auditor Opinion & Independence

The audit was conducted in compliance with International Standards on Auditing (ISA). The independent auditors expressed an unqualified opinion, verifying accurate representation of financial position and internal control integrity.

Overhead Governance Policy

The Board of Directors maintains a mandatory governance policy capping administrative and fundraising expenses at 20% of total expenditure (18.6% achieved in 2025).

Operating Reserve Liquidity

Malkia maintains an operating cash reserve equivalent to 90 days of core operational expenditures, ensuring rescue and safe boarding continuity during humanitarian or environmental emergencies.
